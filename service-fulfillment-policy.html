Service Fulfillment Policy

This policy outlines how SOLUTIONS EA LLC plans, schedules, and delivers demolition and post-construction services.

Highlights

  • Services are fulfilled according to signed scope and project documentation.
  • Scheduling depends on site readiness, access, and safety constraints.
  • Clients are responsible for approved access and required coordination.
  • Completion standards are validated through closeout or walkthrough steps.

Document Details

Effective date March 12, 2026
Applies to Service delivery and closeout process
Contact sales@solutionseallc.com or info@solutionseallc.com

Contents

Scope and Engagement Scheduling and Access Delivery and Completion Client Responsibilities Changes and Delays Contact Information

Effective Date: March 12, 2026

Scope and Engagement

Services are fulfilled according to written proposals, statements of work, accepted estimates, or other signed service documentation. Work outside the agreed scope requires written authorization.

Scheduling and Access

  • Project start and sequencing are scheduled based on confirmed scope, site readiness, and access windows.
  • Work can be phased and coordinated around active operations, tenant occupancy, and safety requirements.
  • Schedule windows may change due to site conditions, permit-related constraints, or third-party dependencies.

Delivery and Completion

  • Work is delivered according to the documented scope, agreed sequencing, and applicable project standards.
  • Closeout may include walkthrough support, punch-list response, and turnover-ready handoff steps where included.
  • Any final acceptance criteria are governed by the signed project agreement.

Client Responsibilities

  • Provide authorized site access and project contacts for coordination.
  • Disclose known constraints that can affect safety, scope, or schedule.
  • Coordinate owner, tenant, GC, or facility approvals required to perform the work.

Changes and Delays

Site conditions, concealed conditions, scope changes, and third-party coordination may affect timeline and fulfillment sequence. We document material impacts and coordinate revised execution steps in writing.
